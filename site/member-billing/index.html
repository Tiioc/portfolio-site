1The problem

At The Paseo Club, I handled dues, refunds and failed payments. I once charged the wrong member because two members had the same name.

2What I made

A Google Sheet that catches billing mistakes and lists what to fix. Claude Code wrote the formulas and script. I set the rules and tested it.

3How it works

  1. Uses member IDsSo two members with the same name stay separate.
  2. Catches mistakesDeclined cards, double charges, unsent refunds.
  3. Makes a fix listWho to call, refund or retry.

4What it found

Each row of the fix list says what to do: call, refund, or retry.

5What I learned

Looking people up by name breaks fast, even with one shared name.

The fine print
  • A made-up club with 36 members and 113 payments. I planted 15 mistakes.

  • A short script can email the fix list at 8am. I haven't run it inside the sheet yet.
