Member Billing Tracker

  • Self-initiated
  • Fake test data
  • v1

A Google Sheet that catches billing mistakes at a members' club and turns them into a daily fix list for the front desk.

The problem

At The Paseo Club I handled monthly dues, failed payments, refunds and disputes in HubSpot. I once charged the wrong member because two members had the exact same name.

What I built

  • Looks up every member by ID, never by name.
  • Flags failed cards, double charges and refunds never sent.
  • Builds a daily fix list: who to call, refund or retry.

What happened

15 / 15

Planted billing problems caught, with 0 false flags

4

Mistakes a look-up-by-name version made on the same data

Tested on fake data made for it, in a real Google Sheet.

What I learned

Looking people up by name breaks fast. A version that did it made 4 mistakes on one shared name: it missed a refund that was still owed, gave one charge the wrong reason and flagged two normal charges.

How I made it

I picked the problem, set the rules and tested everything. Claude Code wrote the code.

The details
  • The fake data. A made-up club with 36 members, August to October 2026: 113 payments and 4 approved refunds. I planted 15 problems: 4 failed cards, 4 charges after a pause or cancel, 2 double charges, 2 refunds never sent, 2 disputes and 1 payment on the wrong account.
  • The same-name test. Two members are both named Chris Morgan, one active and one paused. 5 tests check that each one only gets their own flags and refunds.
  • How it's checked. Every result is compared to the list of planted problems and to a separate version of the rules. LibreOffice and Google Sheets both matched it row for row. 40 checks, all passing.
  • The morning email. A short Apps Script emails the fix list at 8am. It's tested with stand-ins for Google, but I haven't run it inside the sheet yet.
  • The weak spot. It catches 15 of 15 because I planted them. A mistake it has no rule for, like the wrong price, would get through.

Built in plain HTML and CSS with Claude Code, which I directed and fact-checked.
